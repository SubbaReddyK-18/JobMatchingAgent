This document specifies the database underlying the 72-agent platform. It accompanies a 
set of PostgreSQL DDL files that have been executed and tested: 221 tables across 21 
schemas, 457 foreign keys, 431 indexes, and 19 row-level security policies. Everything 
described here runs. 
The document covers the design decisions and their reasons, the schema map, the parts 
that are easy to get wrong, the security model, physical design guidance, and a phased build 
order. An appendix lists every table.

Part A — Design principles 
Eleven decisions shape the whole model. Most of the cost of getting a schema like this 
wrong is concentrated in these. 
A.1 One data layer, not 72 application databases 
Every agent reads and writes through one canonical model. The alternative, where each 
agent owns its own store, produces 72 inconsistent answers to the same question within a 
year. Pass percentage must mean one thing. 
A.2 Course identity and course version are different entities 
A course has a stable identity: its title, its owning department. It also has a 
regulation-specific version carrying credits, contact hours, syllabus units, and course 
outcomes. curriculum.course holds the first; curriculum.course_version holds the 
second. 
This is not fussiness. A typical institution has three or four regulations running 
simultaneously across live batches. A 2022-batch student and a 2025-batch student taking 
a course with the same name are taking different courses with different credits and 
different outcomes. Collapsing them into one row is the single most common cause of 
wrong answers in academic systems, and it is very expensive to unpick later. 
A.3 course_offering is the central grain 
One course_version, in one term, taught to one section. Allocation, timetable, lesson plan, 
class sessions, attendance, assessments, marks and outcome attainment all hang off this. 
If this grain is wrong or ambiguous, every downstream number is ambiguous. It is worth 
spending time confirming that this definition matches how the institution actually operates, 
including edge cases such as merged sections for electives and courses taught jointly by two 
departments. 
A.4 Registration is separate from section membership 
academics.student_registration records who is actually taking an offering. Section 
membership is not sufficient, because electives, backlog re-registration, lateral entry, and 
audit registrations all break the assumption. Attendance denominators and result 
populations both depend on this table being right. 
A.5 Question-level marks are stored, not just totals 
assessment.student_question_mark records each student’s mark on each question, and 
assessment.paper_question tags each question to a course outcome, unit and Bloom level. 
Course outcome attainment cannot be automated without this. Institutions that capture 
only assessment totals discover during accreditation that a year of evidence must be 

--- PAGE 5 ---
reconstructed by hand from physical answer scripts. Capturing question-level marks costs 
almost nothing at entry time if the mark entry screen is designed for it from the start. 
A.6 Derived analytical results are stored, not recomputed 
attendance.attendance_summary, academics.coverage_snapshot, hr.faculty_workload 
and quality.kpi_value are tables, not views. Each row carries as_of_date and the agent 
that computed it. 
The reason is that the history of a derived figure is itself the reportable artefact. Knowing 
that a student’s attendance was 82 percent in week six and 70 percent in week ten is what 
makes the trend and the projection possible. A view that always recomputes from current 
data cannot answer what the position was last month. 
A.7 Every consequential record carries its provenance 
faculty_allocation stores the match score and rationale the optimiser produced, 
alongside the human who approved it and any override reason. co_attainment stores the 
specific question ids that contributed. agent_output stores the reasoning summary and 
citations. risk_flag stores the contributing signals. 
This is what allows a peer team, a contesting faculty member, or an auditor to ask why the 
system said what it said, and get an answer. 
A.8 Access is enforced at the data layer 
Row-level security policies on the sensitive tables mean an agent is technically incapable of 
retrieving another student’s record, regardless of how the question is phrased. 
Prompt-level instructions are not an access control mechanism. 
A.9 Confidential data lives in a separate schema 
Counselling, crisis escalation, and health-related records are in confidential, with its own 
grants and its own encryption. The general application roles have no access at all. 
Disciplinary records sit in studentlife but under their own RLS policy and with a 
mandatory retention_until date. 
Aggregation creates risk that the individual source systems did not carry. The 360-degree 
student profile view deliberately excludes all of this. 
A.10 Configuration is data, not code 
Attainment rubrics, workload norms, fee structures, appraisal rubrics, scholarship 
eligibility criteria, and code lists are all rows. Institutions revise these between cycles, and 
different programmes within one institution frequently use different values. Hard-coding 
any of them guarantees a change request every year.

A.11 Prohibited model inputs are declared, not merely omitted 
agentops.model_version.excluded_features records which attributes a predictive agent 
must not use. Declaring the exclusion means that a later change reintroducing a protected 
attribute, or a proxy for one, is visible in review rather than invisible in a feature list.

Part D — The parts that are easy to get wrong 
D.1 Regulation versioning 
curriculum.regulation carries effective_from_admission_year and 
effective_to_admission_year, plus a superseded_by link. course_version is unique on 
(regulation_id, programme_id, course_code). 
Any query about a student must resolve through their batch to their regulation. A query for 
“the syllabus of CS301” without a regulation context is under-specified and the API should 
reject it rather than guess. 
D.2 Attendance grain and denominators 
attendance.attendance_record is one row per student per class_session. It is 
partitioned by session_date. 
The denominator is the count of class_session rows with status = 'CONDUCTED', not the 
timetable. Reality diverges from the timetable through cancellations, substitutions, 
compensation classes and extra classes, and computing attendance against the plan rather 
than against what happened produces figures that faculty will correctly dispute. 
attendance_summary stores both raw_pct and adjusted_pct, the latter after applying 
on-duty and approved-leave rules. Every report must state which one it is using. It also 
stores trend_slope and projected_end_pct. The projection is the number that changes 
student behaviour; the current percentage only tells them where they are. 
D.3 The evidence chain 
Accreditation requires that a summary figure resolve to its source. The chain is: 
student_question_mark → paper_question (tagged to course_outcome) → 
co_attainment (within an attainment_run bound to a rubric version) → po_attainment 
(weighted by co_po_map.strength) → quality.evidence_item → the submitted report. 
The view outcomes.v_attainment_trace walks this in one query. When a peer team asks 
how a programme outcome attainment of 2.4 was arrived at, the answer should take 
seconds. Retrofitting this traceability is very difficult; it has to be in the model from the 
start. 
co_attainment.contributing_questions stores the actual question ids, so the trail does 
not depend on reconstructing a join. 

--- PAGE 10 ---
D.4 Partial payments and the fee waterfall 
finance.fee_head.allocation_priority defines the order in which a partial payment is 
applied across heads. payment_allocation records where each rupee went. Without an 
explicit waterfall, two people computing the same student’s tuition outstanding will get 
different answers. 
fee_demand.scholarship_expected exists so that reminder suppression is possible. 
Chasing a student for fees a sanctioned scholarship already covers is the most common 
avoidable failure in fee follow-up, and reminder_dispatch.suppressed with its reason 
makes the suppression auditable. 
D.5 Publication deduplication 
The same paper arrives from Scopus, Web of Science and Google Scholar in different forms. 
research.publication holds the resolved record; research.publication_source holds 
one row per source with the raw record, the match method and the match confidence. 
research.affiliation_variant is a small table with outsized effect. A single institution 
appears in the literature under a dozen spellings, and the completeness of this list 
determines the recall of the publication sweep more than anything else in the pipeline. 
D.6 Venue metrics are time-stamped 
research.venue_metric carries checked_at and valid_until per source per year. 
Indexing status changes. Presenting a cached status as current is how a faculty member 
publishes in a journal that was delisted last month.

Part E — The agentops schema 
This is what distinguishes the platform from a reporting system, and it is the part most 
implementations omit. 
E.1 Registry and constraint 
agentops.agent records each agent’s class (1 retrieval, 2 analytical, 3 predictive), its 
reasoning policy, whether it requires human approval, its escalation rule, and its named 
owner. agent_tool declares exactly which schemas and objects it may reach and under 
what row scope. An agent cannot reach data outside its declared tool set. 
Agents without owners degrade silently, so owner_user_id should be treated as mandatory 
in practice even though the column is nullable for bootstrap. 
E.2 Run provenance 
agent_run records every invocation with its trigger, its scope, its latency and its cost. 
agent_run_input records exactly which records fed it. parent_run_id captures 
orchestration chains, so a composite answer from Agent 70 can be decomposed into the 
agent calls that produced it. 
E.3 The human approval gate 
agent_output.requires_approval and approval_status, with human_review recording 
the decision, the modified payload where applicable, and the reason. 
human_review.reason_category is the most valuable field in the schema for improving the 
system. An agent whose recommendations are rejected more than half the time is telling 
you something specific about either its logic or its inputs, and the category tells you which. 
E.4 The detect–decide–act–measure loop 
risk_flag → intervention → intervention_participant → intervention_outcome. 
Three design points. First, risk_flag stores baseline_value and observed_value, because 
the meaningful unit is deviation from a student’s own normal, not distance from a cohort 
average. Second, contributing_signals is required, so the responder receives evidence 
rather than a bare flag. Third, confirmed_by_human is captured on closure, which is what 
makes the false-positive rate measurable and thresholds recalibrable. 
intervention_outcome.comparison_group_delta is the column that separates 
measurement from wishful thinking. Improvement in participants means nothing without a 
comparable group who did not attend. Most institutions run remedial classes for years 
without ever establishing whether they change outcomes; this column costs almost nothing 
and eventually reallocates significant effort. 

--- PAGE 12 ---
E.5 Model governance 
model_version records the feature list, the explicitly excluded features, the training 
window, and holdout accuracy, precision, recall and false-positive rate. No Class 3 agent 
should reach ACTIVE without a validated model version. 
fairness_audit is required each term per Class 3 agent per group attribute, with 
published_internally tracked, because an unpublished audit tends not to be acted on. 
agent_incident covers what happens when an agent produces a harmful or materially 
wrong output, including whether affected people were notified.

Part F — Security model 
Four layers. 
F.1 Roles and scope 
identity.user_role binds a user to a role with an explicit scope: SELF, SECTION, 
COURSE_OFFERING, PROGRAMME, DEPARTMENT, CAMPUS or INSTITUTION. Scope determines 
which rows the user sees, and it is time-boxed with valid_from and valid_to. 
identity.delegation handles temporary transfer of approval authority. 
F.2 Session context 
The application sets, on every connection inside a transaction: 
SET LOCAL app.user_id    = '<uuid>';​
SET LOCAL app.student_id = '<uuid>';   -- when the user is a student​
SET LOCAL app.faculty_id = '<uuid>';​
SET LOCAL app.role_codes = 'FACULTY,MENTOR';​
SET LOCAL app.dept_scope = '<uuid>,<uuid>';​
SET LOCAL app.agent_code = 'A11_ATTENDANCE_ANALYSIS';  -- when an agent acts 
SET LOCAL rather than SET matters: outside a transaction it does not apply, and the policies 
will then correctly return nothing. 
F.3 Row-level security 
Policies are in place on attendance_summary, internal_mark, question_paper, 
counselling_case, counselling_note, disciplinary_case, and risk_flag. Helper 
functions teaches_offering(), is_mentor_of() and student_in_scope() express the 
common predicates.

F.4 Separation and immutability 
confidential is not granted to any general application role. identity.audit_log is 
partitioned by time and protected by a trigger that rejects UPDATE and DELETE. 
hr.faculty_leave_public is a view that masks the medical reason for leave, and 
substitution notifications must read from it rather than the base table. 
studentlife.disciplinary_case.retention_until is enforced in the RLS predicate itself, 
so an expired record becomes invisible even before the expunge job runs. 
F.5 Data protection readiness 
identity.consent records purpose-bound consent with grant and withdrawal timestamps. 
identity.data_request handles access, correction, erasure and consent-withdrawal 
requests with a due date. people.person.social_category carries a comment restricting 
it to statutory reporting and fairness auditing. 
Verify the current position under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act with legal 
counsel rather than relying on this document; the rules and enforcement position have 
been developing.

G.2 Partitioning 
Two tables are partitioned by range on date: 
•​
attendance.attendance_record by session_date 
•​
identity.audit_log by occurred_at 
Yearly partitions are provided for 2026 and 2027. Add partitions ahead of time, ideally 
through pg_partman or a scheduled job, because an insert with no matching partition fails. 
Consider partitioning assessment.student_question_mark by term once it passes roughly 
10 million rows. 
Note that on a partitioned table the primary key must include the partition column, which 
is why these two tables have composite primary keys.

G.3 Indexing 
431 indexes are defined. The patterns worth knowing: 
•​
Partial indexes on hot predicates. idx_backlog_open covers only status = 
'PENDING'; idx_qbank_lookup covers only validated question bank items; 
idx_alert_unread covers only unread alerts. These are much smaller and much 
faster than full indexes for the queries that actually run. 
•​
Trigram indexes for fuzzy text. pg_trgm GIN indexes on person.full_name, 
venue.name, publication.title, policy_clause.clause_text and 
document_chunk.chunk_text. These support the name matching and clause 
searching that agents do constantly. 
•​
Deadline indexes. idx_patent_due, idx_phd_milestone_due, idx_call_deadline 
and idx_compliance_status all support the scheduled sweeps that generate alerts. 
G.4 Vector search 
knowledge.chunk_embedding.embedding is declared as real[] so that the schema loads 
without extensions. In production, install pgvector and change it: 
CREATE EXTENSION vector;​
ALTER TABLE knowledge.chunk_embedding​
  ALTER COLUMN embedding TYPE vector(1536) USING embedding::vector;​
CREATE INDEX ON knowledge.chunk_embedding​
  USING hnsw (embedding vector_cosine_ops); 
At the volumes above, pgvector inside the same PostgreSQL instance is sufficient. A 
separate vector database is unnecessary complexity for a knowledge base of this size, and 
keeping embeddings alongside the relational data makes permission filtering on retrieval 
much simpler. 
Retrieval must filter on document.is_retrievable_by_agents and on sensitivity against 
the requesting role, before the vector search rather than after. 
G.5 Analytics separation 
Operational agents read from the transactional schemas. Analytical and reporting agents, 
particularly 10, 20, 34, 38, 63, 71 and 72, should read from a separate read replica or a 
nightly-refreshed star schema. 
A minimal warehouse for this platform needs conformed dimensions on student, faculty, 
course version, term, department, programme and section, with fact tables for attendance, 
assessment, results, publications, placements and finance. Grain each fact at the level its 
source table already uses so that the mapping stays trivial. 
Do not build the warehouse in phase one. Build it when dashboard queries begin to 
compete with transactional load, which for an institution of this size is typically in year two.

Part H — Data ingestion and quality 
H.1 The ingestion gate 
attendance.ingestion_batch is the pattern to replicate for every bulk source. It records 
the source, the row counts, the validation report, and a status that must reach ACCEPTED 
before downstream analysis runs. 
Agent 11 must not analyse a batch in FAILED or PARTIAL state. Silently analysing corrupt 
data is worse than producing nothing, because the output looks plausible. 
Validation for attendance specifically: sessions recorded as conducted match the timetable 
or an explained variance; no student marked present in two simultaneous periods; every 
roll number resolves against the student master; total classes held is consistent within a 
section. 
H.2 Document extraction confidence 
knowledge.extracted_field.confidence gates entry into academic and financial records. 
Below the configured threshold the field routes to NEEDS_REVIEW and a human confirms it. 
Optical character recognition on scanned certificates and marks cards has a material error 
rate, and a transposed digit in a roll number attaches one student’s result to another. 
admissions.application_document.verification_status follows the same principle: 
AUTO_FLAGGED never means rejected, and only a human sets REJECTED. 
H.3 Identity resolution 
people.person_identifier maps each person to their identifier in each source system, 
with a uniqueness constraint on (system_code, identifier). 
people.person.merged_into handles duplicate resolution without deleting history. 
Expect this to be the single largest effort in phase zero. Institutions routinely find that the 
same student exists under different identifiers in the ERP, the biometric system and the 
examination system, and that reconciling them surfaces genuine data errors that have been 
latent for years. 
H.4 Anomaly capture 
assessment.mark_anomaly records detected problems with an explicit status lifecycle, so 
that an anomaly is either explained, corrected, or dismissed with a reason rather than 
quietly ignored. The same pattern would serve well for attendance and finance if those 
areas prove noisy.

Part I — Build order 
The schema is modular and the files are numbered in dependency order. Deploy in phases 
matching the agent roadmap. 
Phase 0 — Foundation 
Files 01, 02, 03, 11. 
Schemas: core, people, identity, curriculum, plus the security layer. 
Work: integration pipelines from existing systems, identity resolution, three years of 
historical data cleaning, single sign-on, and the role and permission model. No agents ship 
in this phase, which is why it gets skipped and why skipping it is the most expensive 
available mistake. 
Exit criterion: a validated curriculum structure for every live regulation, a resolved person 
register, and one demonstration query proving the pipeline works end to end. 
Phase 1 — Retrieval agents 
Files 09 (partial: governance, knowledge), 12. 
Adds the document store, policy documents and clauses, circulars, and the read views. 
Supports Agents 1, 2, 53, 55, 65. 
Exit criterion: students and faculty getting cited, correct answers to natural language 
questions. 
Phase 2 — Core analytics 
Files 04, 05. 
Adds academics, attendance, assessment, exams, outcomes. Supports Agents 6, 8, 10, 11, 
33, 34. 
This is the largest single increment and the one that delivers the most operational value. 
Attendance analysis runs weekly and touches everyone; outcome attainment removes the 
largest manual burden in accreditation work. 
Exit criterion: weekly automated attendance analysis with intervention lists, and course 
outcome attainment computed rather than compiled. 
Phase 3 — Operations and research 
File 06, plus hr from file 08. 
Adds research, engagement, hr. Supports Agents 3, 4, 17–29, 58–61. The research block is 
largely independent of the student data work and can proceed in parallel with a separate 
team.

Phase 4 — Predictive and composite 
File 10. 
Adds agentops in full. Supports Agents 12, 14, 15, 16, 69, 70. 
Do not attempt this before four terms of clean data exist. Every model must be validated 
against a held-out historical cohort, recorded in model_version, and audited in 
fairness_audit before it influences a real decision.

The smoke test creates one institution, one department, one programme under regulation 
R23, one course with two units and two course outcomes mapped to a programme 
outcome, two students, one faculty member, one offering, attendance summaries, a 
mid-term with question-level marks, and a computed attainment chain. It then walks a 
programme outcome level back to the individual question ids that produced it. 
Do not load 99_smoke_test.sql into a production database. 
J.3 What is deliberately not included 
No triggers computing business logic. Attendance percentages, internal marks and 
attainment levels are computed by the agents and written as rows, not derived by database 
triggers. Trigger-computed business logic is difficult to test, difficult to change, and invisible 
to the people who need to understand it. 
No historisation on every table. Only where the history is genuinely used: derived 
analytical snapshots, regulation and policy versioning, mark change logs, and the audit trail. 
Universal temporal tables would double the model’s size for little benefit. 
No warehouse star schema. Deferred to phase two or later, as described in G.5. 
Sizing of text columns. PostgreSQL text has no performance penalty over varchar(n). 
Where a business rule genuinely constrains length, add a CHECK. 
J.4 Open decisions for the institution 
Five things the schema accommodates but cannot decide: 
1.​
Internal mark formula. assessment.internal_mark.formula_version and 
components support any formula. The actual rule, including best-of-two handling 
and rounding, needs a written specification before Agent 33 is built. 
2.​
Attainment rubric. Threshold, level bands and direct/indirect weighting go in 
outcomes.attainment_rubric. Different programmes may need different rubrics. 
3.​
Workload weights. hr.workload_norm.activity_weights needs the institution’s 
actual conversion factors for lab hours, project supervision and administrative roles. 
4.​
Audit log granularity. Logging every read produces 20 million rows a year and 
genuine forensic capability. Logging only writes and sensitive reads produces a 
fraction of that. Decide deliberately. 
5.​
Retention periods. disciplinary_case.retention_until has no default. Set an 
institutional policy rather than leaving it to whoever creates the record.
